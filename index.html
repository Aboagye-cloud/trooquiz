Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Here is what students ask most.

What is Trooquiz?
Trooquiz is an online quiz platform where students practise STEM subjects, with questions built around the GES curriculum, and are marked immediately after each quiz.
Do I need to create an account?
No. Students use the site as guests. Tap I am a student, choose your level and subject, and start.
How does a quiz work?
Each session has 15 random questions from the subject's question bank, with 60 seconds for each question. If the time runs out, the question counts as unanswered and the quiz moves on.
How is my score worked out, and what is the pass mark?
Your score is shown out of 100%. A score of 50% or more is a pass, and anything below 50% is a fail. With 15 questions, that means at least 8 correct answers.
When do I see my result?
Straight away. As soon as you answer the last question, your score and pass or fail result appear on screen.
Can I retake a quiz?
Yes, as many times as you like. Each attempt draws a new random set of questions from the bank.
Can I use Trooquiz on my phone?
Yes. The site adapts to phones, tablets and computers.
